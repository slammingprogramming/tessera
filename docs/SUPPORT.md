help, the options below are entirely optional. Nothing in the program asks for money or phones home.

Notes before you send anything:

- **The Bitcoin address is a Silent Payment address** (it starts with `sp1`, [BIP 352](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0352.mediawiki)),
  not an ordinary `bc1`/`1`/`3` address. Only a wallet that supports Silent Payments can pay it; if your wallet rejects it,
  use another coin or Ko-fi.
- Copy the address from this page or the repository, and compare the first and last characters afterwards. Crypto payments
  cannot be reversed, and look-alike addresses are a common trick.
- Send a small test amount first if you are unsure.
- Tessera never asks for payment inside the game or by message. Anyone who contacts you in the maintainer's name and asks you to
  send money somewhere else is not the maintainer.
- Donations do not buy features, priority or support, and they are not tax-deductible.
