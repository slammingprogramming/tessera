Users and security (auth)
The auth package adds accounts and a login screen for computers other people can reach.

  useradd <name> [--admin]    the first account is always an administrator
  passwd [name]               change a password
  users                       list accounts
  su <name>                   a shell as someone else (needs their password)
  sudo <command>              run one command unrestricted (administrators)
  lock                        hide the screen until your password is typed
  usermod <name> --role user|admin|guest | --lock | --unlock
  userdel <name>

Without accounts the computer is open. Once one exists, the login screen appears at boot and after you leave the shell.
Ordinary users can write only to their home folder and /tmp and cannot read other homes.

IMPORTANT: this is a courtesy, not a vault. A ComputerCraft computer is one Lua program that anyone at the keyboard can
rewrite, and anyone who can break the computer can read its disk. The checks keep honest players from accidents and casual
snooping; they do not stop a determined one. Protect valuable computers physically (claims, locked areas).
