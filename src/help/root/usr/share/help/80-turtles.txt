Turtles
Tessera remembers where a turtle is even when its chunk unloads in the middle of a move, and can resume jobs afterwards.

Jobs (install the packages you need)
  bore, carve, stripmine, quarry   digging jobs; each takes sizes as arguments and saves progress
  build                            builds from a plan; treefarm, cropfarm, cobblegen, guard
  tstatus                          position, fuel, current job;  tresume  continues an interrupted job
  tlisten / tcontrol               control a turtle from another computer with a shared passphrase

Getting around: the pathfinder (tsr.turtle.nav) plans routes around known obstacles, learns new ones by bumping into
them, and can dig through when no way round exists.

Fleets: with the fleet package, `fleet add <turtle>`, `fleet list` shows position, fuel and job of every turtle,
`fleet run all quarry 16 16` starts a job on all of them, `fleet goto <turtle> x y z` sends one somewhere.
Turtles only run jobs from a short whitelist and only for controllers they allow (`fleet allow <controller>` on the turtle).

Tip: with a GPS network in range, a turtle can calibrate its coordinates to the world (see the gps package).
